# Recruiter review improvements — 15 October 2026

The portfolio now presents the candidate's role, qualification, location and CV before the interactive hero. A separate recruiter overview gathers relevant skills, project evidence, experience, research and direct contact links in a quieter layout that can be printed.

Project search and role filters help reviewers find relevant work. Project titles and taglines stay complete. Each case study adds an engineering decision, code/test-source links, a review scenario, explicit scope and a next step. Evidence links use reviewed snapshots where available. The original portrait, CV bytes, artwork, palette, themes and decorative motion are preserved.

The EpigraphiX-AI wording reflects the inspected public Python engine: simulated attention and deterministic character mapping. The research publication remains linked separately, without presenting its benchmark claims as independently reproduced software results. AegisForge-X heuristics and Deciphera simulations are also labeled accurately.

Useful future additions require real evidence: actual application screenshots or a short authored demo, dated evaluation datasets/results, measured project outcomes and verifiable credential links. Add those when available; do not replace them with invented metrics, testimonials or acceptance percentages.

No portfolio can establish a 90–100% probability of company acceptance. Hiring also depends on role fit, qualifications, interview performance and the employer's selection process.
# New interactive capabilities — 15 October 2026

Added two independent interactive pages, reachable from the homepage and recruiter overview:

1. **Requirement-to-evidence explorer:** paste technical requirements or choose a role preset; inspect recognized terms, linked projects and missing project evidence.
2. **Project comparison:** choose up to three projects, compare scope/stack/decisions/validation/limits and share a URL containing only the selection.
3. **Application project brief:** add an optional company and role, then copy, download a text file, or print/save PDF with selected project evidence and original CV link.
4. **One-time-access race experiment:** step or run schedules for 2, 5 or 25 readers; switch between separate reads/writes and atomic claims; explore expired and revoked states.
5. **SHA-256 integrity playground:** edit local evidence text, compare real digests, establish a new baseline and reset the sample.

The brief does not invent experience. The matcher is a transparent term catalog, without hiring scores. The race is explicitly a model; the hash computation runs in the visitor's browser. Interactive inputs are not saved or uploaded.
