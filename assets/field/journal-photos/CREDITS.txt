Photo editions
The seven Education and Internships photo-edition films reuse these photographs
as their complete visual track, scaled and placed on a matte without altering
the photographs themselves. Their original portfolio narration is unchanged.
Photographs in the editions keep the licenses above. The GEC Thrissur photograph
shows the actual campus. All other photographs in the editions are contextual
photographs, not authenticated footage of the named schools or companies.
