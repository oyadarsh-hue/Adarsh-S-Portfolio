Portfolio film audio descriptions

The descriptions were written for the portfolio artwork and rendered as synthetic speech.
The English film transcripts were extracted from the existing video caption tracks.

The Kuwaiti Arabic descriptions have separate Kuwaiti wording and use the same Arabic voice as the standard Arabic descriptions.
Tamil is read by the Rasa Female neural voice published by tinisoft under CC BY 4.0, trained on AI4Bharat Rasa.
https://huggingface.co/tinisoft/piper-ta_IN-rasa_female-medium
https://huggingface.co/datasets/ai4bharat/Rasa
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Sinhala uses chan4lk’s Sinhala Medium neural voice, published under MIT and trained on the OpenSLR 30 Sinhala corpus.
https://huggingface.co/chan4lk/piper-tts-sinhala
https://www.openslr.org/30/

Amharic uses eSpeak NG.
https://github.com/espeak-ng/espeak-ng

Spanish voice data is credited to OHF-Voice. Bengali sources include OpenSLR 37 and the CMU Indic corpus.
https://www.openslr.org/37/
https://festvox.org/cmu_indic/
Hindi voice training is credited to PravalX and the AI4Bharat Indic corpus.
