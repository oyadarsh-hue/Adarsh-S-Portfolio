EpigraphiX-AI

A neural OCR and epigraphical-intelligence suite for degraded historical palm-leaf manuscripts.

Overview

EpigraphiX-AI applies image restoration, segmentation, neural OCR, classical machine learning and NLP to help analyze difficult Malayalam and Grantha palm-leaf manuscript imagery.

Highlights

  • Neural OCR workflows using TrOCR and CNN-based components.
  • Image processing for low-contrast and degraded manuscript surfaces.
  • Machine-learning classification and linguistic post-processing.
  • Document-intelligence and vector PDF reporting workflows.
