Deciphera

A semantic ledger and covenant auditor that turns complex Bitcoin off-chain state transitions into human-readable audit narratives.

Overview

Deciphera is designed around Bitcoin Layer-2 transaction interpretation, covenant inspection and semantic reporting for users, technical reviewers and compliance-oriented workflows.

Highlights

  • Semantic narrative generation for off-chain state transitions.
  • Bitcoin Layer-2 and covenant-oriented visualization concepts.
  • Multiple levels of explanation for user, auditor and technical perspectives.
  • Interactive modern web dashboard architecture.
