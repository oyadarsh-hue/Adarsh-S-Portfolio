AssentTag

A privacy-aware image sharing and biometric identity framework focused on privacy-by-default face protection.

Overview

AssentTag combines Django web engineering with computer vision to detect faces in uploaded media, verify identity and selectively obscure faces according to privacy rules.

Highlights

  • Privacy-by-default face detection and selective blurring.
  • ResNet-based facial embeddings and Euclidean-distance verification.
  • Django/MySQL application workflows with authentication and audit features.
  • Responsive web interface for privacy-aware media sharing.
